# 08 · Salon Booking Website

**Context:** Part of my freelance web design work: I build websites for local businesses with no online presence, then pitch them to the owner in person.

## The project

A website for a Pretoria hair salon with:

- **Online booking** across **six stylists**, so clients can choose who they see and when.
- An **owner dashboard** to view and manage bookings.
- A design that presents the business professionally on mobile and desktop.

## What I learned

- Building for a real business means starting from what the owner needs (fewer missed calls, a full diary), not from features.
- Booking systems handle personal information, so data protection (POPIA) applies even to a small salon site.

**Tools:** HTML · CSS · JavaScript
