# 07 · Phishing Incident Response Plan

**Context:** Group assessment at CTU built around a scenario: a fictional company, SecureTech Solutions, hit by an **AI-generated phishing campaign**.

## My role

- **Group leader** (Group 4).
- When group members dropped out, I took on two of the core tasks myself:
  - **Task 1 · Threat Identification:** how AI makes phishing more convincing, the indicators that still give it away, and the assets at risk.
  - **Task 3 · Incident Response Plan:** preparation, detection and analysis, containment, eradication, recovery and lessons learned.
- Delivered a **20-slide** final presentation.

## What I learned

- An IR plan has to name who does what; a list of phases isn't a plan.
- AI-written phishing removes the old spelling-mistake tells, so detection has to rely on sender verification, links and context.
- Keeping a team delivering when people drop out is part of the job.

**Skills:** Incident response planning · Threat analysis · Leadership · Presenting
