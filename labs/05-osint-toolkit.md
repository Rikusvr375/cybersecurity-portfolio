# 05 · OSINT Toolkit & Teaching Guide

**Goal:** Build a ready-to-use open-source intelligence toolkit on Kali and turn it into teaching material for classmates.

## Toolkit

| Category | Tools |
|---|---|
| Username & account discovery | Sherlock · Maigret · Blackbird |
| Email & phone intelligence | Holehe · Ignorant · PhoneInfoga |
| Domain & infrastructure recon | theHarvester · Subfinder · Recon-ng |
| File metadata | exiftool |

## Teaching material

- An **interactive HTML walkthrough** of Recon-ng: workspaces, modules, and building up findings step by step.
- A **36-page compiled PDF guide** covering installation and use of every tool in the kit.
- Used in an **instructor-authorised OSINT lab exercise**.

## What I learned

- How much can be found from public sources alone, and therefore how much organisations should limit what they expose.
- OSINT has clear ethical and legal limits (POPIA in South Africa); it must be scoped and authorised.
- Explaining a tool to others is the fastest way to find the gaps in your own understanding.

**Tools:** Kali Linux · Recon-ng · Sherlock · Maigret · Holehe · theHarvester · Subfinder · PhoneInfoga · Ignorant · Blackbird · exiftool
